# EcoCart catalogue data notes

This educational catalogue uses real-world product/brand names as examples. It is a **curated static demo catalogue**, not a bulk export from a retailer and not a live marketplace feed. The prices and stock quantities are illustrative values for demonstrating catalogue search, filtering, sorting, cart, checkout, and Oracle queries; they must not be presented as current retailer prices or actual inventory. Product names are included to make the demo realistic, but the project does not claim endorsement by any brand.

## Public reference datasets for future expansion

- BigBasket product datasets have been published by community data-analysis projects, including repositories describing product, brand, category, sale-price, market-price, and rating columns. Dataset provenance, redistribution rights, freshness, and any original site terms must be checked before bundling a particular CSV. Reference: https://github.com/veer-bajpai/BigBasket-Exploratory-Data-Analysis--EDA-
- Open Food Facts provides open product data for packaged foods under ODbL/DbCL and product images generally under CC BY-SA. Its contributors do not guarantee data accuracy or completeness; follow attribution and share-alike obligations before reusing it. References: https://openfoodfacts.github.io/openfoodfacts-server/api/ and https://openfoodfacts.github.io/openfoodfacts-server/api/tutorials/license-be-on-the-legal-side/
- OpenMRP describes an openly licensed India-focused product database (ODbL 1.0) with product data dumps, with attribution/share-alike obligations. Check current availability and licensing before using a dump: https://github.com/openmrp-in/openmrp

## Sustainability values

EcoCart Eco-Scores in this demo are **illustrative estimates** computed from assumed material, packaging, recyclability, and durability attributes. They are not certifications, measured carbon footprints, or verified claims about these exact SKUs. The local SVG images are category illustrations rather than photographs of the listed products.
