How the Eco-Score works

Every score on EcoCart is produced by one documented formula ({{ methodology }}) implemented both in the application and inside Oracle as FN_COMPUTE_ECO_SCORE, so the two can never disagree.

The formula

eco_score = 0.40 × material + 0.25 × packaging + 0.20 × recyclability + 0.15 × durability

Material (40%)
Assumed relative impact class of the main raw material — e.g. jute, bamboo and recycled fabric score high; virgin plastic, nylon and mixed electronics score low.
Packaging (25%)
How minimal and recyclable the retail packaging is — from no packaging (100) to plastic blister packs (10).
Recyclability (20%)
Share of the product that can realistically be recycled at end of life.
Durability (15%)
Expected useful life and reusability; single-use items score close to zero.
